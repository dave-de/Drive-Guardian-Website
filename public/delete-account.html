Request deletion of your DriveGuardian account and data

You can request that your DriveGuardian account and all associated data be permanently deleted at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@driveguardian.app with the subject "Delete my DriveGuardian account".
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in (or include that address in the message) so we can verify the request.
  3. We will confirm and permanently delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (name, email, sign-in)
  • All driving activity events and safety scores
  • Location history and live-location data
  • Seatbelt-check photos
  • Family-group memberships and accountability partners
  • Device push-notification tokens

What may be kept

We may retain minimal records where required by law (for example, a log of the deletion request itself), which are never used for any other purpose.

See our Privacy Policy for full details on how your data is handled.
